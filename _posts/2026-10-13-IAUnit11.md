Individual Project: Academic Research Tool
--

The individual implementation of the multi-agent AI academic research tool designed by Group C in our course. The planning agent takes the user's prompt and breaks it down into terms for the retrieval agent. The retrieval agent calls APIs to find relevant articles. The processing agent filters, ranks and summarizes academic articles. The storage agent outputs the final results in markdown. The research results will display an overview, summary of articles, ranked list and references in Harvard Cite Them Right format. Ollama is used to power the LLMs used for these agents. Deepseek, Gemma, Qwen and Llama local models are used. ArXiv, OpenAlex and Core are the APIs. More details can be found in the ZIP file. 

Download the code: [academicresearchtool.zip](/docs/IntelligentAgents/academicresearchtool.zip)

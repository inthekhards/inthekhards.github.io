## The Rise of Intelligence Agents

Artificial Intelligence Agents (AI Agents) are tools that can remember tasks, use one or more AI models, make decisions and act autonomously when doing the jobs that they were programmed to do (Boston Consulting Group, 2026). They are digital tools that can operate without needing much supervision from humans. AI agents collect data, observe and analyze their environment and user interactions, evaluate based off the information they have and act on it. An AI agent could autonomously do research, send emails or reminders, present diagrams or write articles for the tasks they have been assigned to do. An AI agent would be accessing the internet with a browser, an organization’s CRM, client contact list, or cloud file sharing system to do their work. The AI agent goes beyond the idea of a robot doing what they were programmed to do and acts more like an employee.

The use of an AI agent in organizations is rising because it can assist humans in work or be good enough to replace some jobs altogether. Intelligence agents can be applied in a variety of use cases across marketing, sales, IT, supply chain management, software development and administration (Hayes and Downie, 2025). An intelligence agent can research trends, create AI content and captions and post for a company’s social media and website, for example. McKinsey refers to the integration of agentic AI and human works as the “agentic organization” (Sukharevsky et al., 2025). It is predicted that AI agents will be assisting customers and workers in every industry in the future. McKinsey says that organizations that adopt AI agents early can achieve an advantage over their competition in terms of automation, decision-making, efficiency, reducing business costs and hyper-personalizing their products and services to customers.
